# Enemy smoothness: far creatures sleep (polish pass step 7)

**Asked:** step 7 of [[perf-polish]] (priority 0: lag). The user said go.

## Results (Studio playtest, one player in town)
| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Orcs simulated and sent to clients | 120 (24 packs), all moving | **0**: all 120 asleep, anchored |
| Wild animals simulated | 95 | **9** (the town's tame ones, near the player) |
| A sleeping pack's AI | every 0.2–0.3 s per orc and per pack | **once a second for the whole pack** |

## What changed
- New server [Dormancy](../../src/server/Dormancy.luau): creatures far from every player sleep.
  - Asleep: it stops where it stands and its root is anchored once it's on the ground. The server
    spends no physics on it and sends no movement to clients.
  - Its AI only checks once a second whether to wake. Its animations keep playing (an idle still breathes).
  - A player within **220 studs** wakes it; further than **280** from every player puts it back
    to sleep. The gap stops it flickering at the edge.
  - Waking un-anchors it and gives the server back its network ownership (anchoring drops it).
  - Nothing can hit a sleeper: no weapon reaches past 140 studs (CombatConfig missiles).
- [EnemyService](../../src/server/EnemyService.luau):
  - **Packs** sleep as a whole, and only while roaming. Never while hunting or walking home after
    giving up. A hit (`hurtBy`) wakes them too.
  - **Lone orcs** (dungeon patrols, single spawns) sleep the same way, unless chasing. Losing
    health while asleep wakes them.
  - Sparring orcs (owned by a player) never sleep.
- [AnimalService](../../src/server/AnimalService.luau): animals sleep the same way, but never while
  fleeing. A scare (being hit, or a hurt herd mate) wakes them.
- Villagers (9, in town) and the boss are left as they are.

## Verified by Claude (Studio, console-only playtest)
- With the player in town: 120/120 orcs and 86/95 animals asleep; the 9 awake were the town's pens.
- Moving the player next to a pack: within 3 s, 17 orcs nearby were awake, back under server
  ownership, 11 moving, and attacking (the player took damage).
- Back in town: all 120 asleep again within 10 s.
- No script errors.
- Testing gotcha: moving a character from the **server** doesn't work (the client owns it and snaps
  it back). Move it from the client.

## Open
- Tuning: `Dormancy.Config` (WakeRange, SleepRange, Tick).
- If a far player can see a pack (streaming radius up to ~1024 studs), it stands still instead of
  roaming. Packs already pause between roams, so that should look natural.
