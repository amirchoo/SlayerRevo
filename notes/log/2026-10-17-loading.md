**Asked:** step 8 of [[perf-polish]] (priority 0: lag). The user said go.

## What changed
- **A loading screen** ([src/first/LoadingScreen.client.luau](../../src/first/LoadingScreen.client.luau),
  in ReplicatedFirst via `default.project.json`):
  - It replaces Roblox's default one: "Slayer Revo" in the intro's Fondamento gold over a dark warm
    panel, with a gold line and a glint sweeping along it, plus a status line.
  - The status reads "Loading the world", then "Finding your way", then "Gathering your surroundings".
  - It fades out once three things are ready: the game has loaded, your character has spawned,
    and the area round it has streamed in (`RequestStreamAroundAsync`, up to 8 s).
  - It shows for 1 s at least and gives up waiting after 20 s.
  - It's self-contained (no shared modules: they may not have arrived yet). It sits over
    everything, the intro included.
- **Sound preload** ([Sounds](../../src/shared/Sounds.luau) `preload`):
  - It waits until the game has loaded and your character is in, plus 3 s, so it doesn't compete
    with the world while you join.
  - The 6 long ambience loops (`Loop = true`: birdsong, crickets, campfire, wind, cave hum,
    trickle) aren't preloaded. Soundscape fades them in, so a late start doesn't show.
  - Grimgorg's 10 voice and attack sounds load 20 s after the rest.
  - The other ~57 effects load in the first wave.
- **"Load far content later":** already covered, no new code.
  - Streaming (step 4) only sends what's near you.
  - The dungeon layout sits in ServerStorage, which never reaches clients.
  - Far creatures sleep (step 7).
