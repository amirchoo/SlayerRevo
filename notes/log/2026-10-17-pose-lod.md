# Animation smoothness (polish pass step 6)

**Asked:** step 6 of [[perf-polish]] (priority 0: lag). The user OK'd going ahead. From now on
device checks are batched into one at the end of the pass (see [[perf-polish-handoff]]).

## Decision: cheaper Lua posing, not clips
The plan said "replace per-frame joint posing with clips". That would mean making and
publishing new animations for the animals, villagers and pets, a Studio job for the user (scripts
can't publish). Instead the Lua posing stays and gets cheaper with **level of detail**: the
creatures look the same up close, and the far and off-screen ones cost much less. No new
assets, nothing for the user to publish. Clips can still come later if it's needed.

## What changed
- New client [PoseLod](../../src/client/PoseLod.luau): how often a creature is posed.
  | Where (studs from the camera, at PoseDetail 1) | Posed |
  |---|---|
  | on screen, within 45 | every frame |
  | on screen, 45–90 | 30 times a second |
  | on screen, further | 15 times a second |
  | off screen (with a 15% margin) | 6 times a second |
  - A skipped creature's time adds up, so its walk cycle and clips move on at the right speed. A
    step is capped at 0.5 s so it doesn't jump a whole stride.
  - Each creature starts its timer at a random point, so they don't all pose on the same frame.
- [QualityConfig](../../src/shared/Config/QualityConfig.luau) `PoseDetail` scales those distances:
  Low 0.6, Medium 0.85, High 1.
- [AnimalAnimator](../../src/client/AnimalAnimator.luau), [VillagerAnimator](../../src/client/VillagerAnimator.luau)
  and [PetAnimator](../../src/client/PetAnimator.luau) use it, inside their existing ranges (160, 150, 150).
- [DamageFeedback](../../src/client/DamageFeedback.luau) (a leftover from the audit): the low-health
  colour grade is only written when it changes, not every frame at full health.

### Checked, no change
- **Hitstop (HitFeel):** the handoff worried it could leave a track frozen. It can't:
  - `AnimationTrack:Play()` always sets the track's speed (1 by default), so a track that stopped
    during the freeze plays at normal speed next time.
  - A track another script changed mid-freeze (e.g. the staff charge holding its pose) is left
    alone on purpose.
- **Priorities and fades:** consistent. Idles are Idle/Movement, attacks and hit reactions
  Action/Action2, fades 0.05–0.15 s. How the attacks *feel* belongs to the main issue.
- **StaffClient, DashEffects, CookingPose:** they only pose while charging, dashing or cooking, and
  they're cheap. Left as they are.
- **BossAnimator:** one boss, which should stay smooth. Left every frame.
